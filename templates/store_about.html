About LocalDrop

A Local Supplier Dropshipping Marketplace Platform

LocalDrop demonstrates an end-to-end Data Warehouse Management (DWM) architecture, bridging operational e-commerce transactions with dimensional business intelligence and machine learning.

Platform Overview

Decentralized regional dropshipping marketplace

Traditional dropshipping platforms rely heavily on distant overseas fulfillment networks, causing long delivery cycles and high return rates. LocalDrop restructures this model by connecting verified regional Indian manufacturers directly with retail channels across 12 states.

The platform is separated into two synchronized environments:

  • Public Storefront (/store): Customer-facing product catalog, category discovery, and local supplier profiles backed by the operational database (operational.db).
  • Analytics Portal: Back-office dimensional warehouse (warehouse.db), Star & Snowflake schemas, 15 analytical SQL queries, OLAP operations, and Machine Learning mining engines.

DWM Technical Architecture & Methodology

End-to-end data lifecycle from operational OLTP to analytical OLAP

Open Analytics Portal
1. Operational Database

5 relational tables (CUSTOMERS, SUPPLIERS, PRODUCTS, ORDERS, SALES) in 3NF capturing realistic dropshipping records.

2. Automated ETL & Cleansing

Deduplication, string trimming, bounds clamping, ISO date standardization, and IQR statistical outlier detection.

3. Dimensional Warehouse

Star Schema, normalized Snowflake Schema, and Fact Constellation with conformed dimensions and analytical Data Marts.

4. Data Mining & ML

Supervised profitability classification (Decision Tree vs. Naive Bayes), K-Means clustering, and Apriori association rules.
