1. Star Schema Architecture

Central sales records surrounded by customer, product, supplier, and date dimensions

Star Schema

How it works: A star schema organizes data like the points of a star around a central sales table. Because customer, product, and supplier details are kept directly connected without extra sub-tables, business reports and dashboards run quickly and are simple to interpret.

2. Snowflake Schema Architecture

Extended dimensional structure with category and location sub-tables

Snowflake Schema

How it works: A snowflake schema branches out further by splitting categories and geographic cities into dedicated sub-tables. This eliminates repeated text values and saves storage, though answering business questions requires linking more tables together.

4. Fact Constellation (Shared Dimensions)

FACT_SALES and FACT_ORDERS sharing conformed dimensions

A Fact Constellation contains multiple fact tables sharing conformed dimensions. In LocalDrop, FACT_SALES captures financial line-item settlements, while FACT_ORDERS captures operational order status lifecycles. Both share DIM_CUSTOMER, DIM_PRODUCT, DIM_SUPPLIER, and DIM_DATE without duplication.

5. Dedicated Sales Data Mart

Analytical views accelerating reporting queries

  • DM_MONTHLY_CATEGORY_SALES: Aggregated turnover and margin by year, month, and category.
  • DM_SUPPLIER_SCORECARD: Fulfillment count, total revenue, and margin by supplier.
  • DM_CUSTOMER_RFM: Recency, Frequency, and Monetary metrics for customer analysis.
  • DM_CITY_PERFORMANCE: Geographic sales breakdown across 26 Indian urban centers.
